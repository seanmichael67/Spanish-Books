¡Hola! In Spanish-speaking classrooms, children learn to read with syllables: first the five vowels, then ma-me-mi-mo-mu. Spanish is read almost exactly as it is written, so this works fast.

This week's books: Las Vocales (The Vowels) and Sílabas con M (Syllables with M).

Try at home (2 minutes): clap the syllables together. «ma-no» is two claps, «ma-ri-po-sa» is four. Then press 🎤 in the book so your child can say each word after the voice.
