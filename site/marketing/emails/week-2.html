¡Hola! This week, try a real little story: El gatito tiene hambre (The Kitten Is Hungry). A hungry kitten looks for food, and a girl helps.

The three-read trick: read the same story three times this week. The first time, just listen. The second time, press 🎤 Mi turno so your child repeats each sentence. The third time, stop before the last word and let your child finish it.

Ask while you read: «¿Tiene hambre el gatito?» (Is the kitten hungry?) «¿Qué come?» (What does it eat?)
