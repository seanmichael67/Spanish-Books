¡Hola! Colours are the easiest words to practise all day long. This week: El Rojo (The Color Red), El Azul (The Color Blue), El Amarillo (The Color Yellow) and El Verde (The Color Green).

Colour hunt (5 minutes): after reading, ask «¿Dónde está algo rojo?» (Where is something red?) and race to find it. Swap roles: your child asks, you find.

Tip: in the books, tap any word to hear it on its own. Colour words come back in many other books too.
